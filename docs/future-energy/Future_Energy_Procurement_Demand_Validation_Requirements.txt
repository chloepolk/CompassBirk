PROSERA COMPASS 
FUTURE ENERGY
Procurement Demand Validation
Product: Compass procurement workflow
Audience: Forward engineer and product engineering team
Status: Requested prototype change
Scope: Check whether a purchase is necessary before optimizing how the organization buys it
Required outcome  Before an ITT is issued or an award is submitted for approval, Compass must identify whether suitable owned inventory can satisfy all or part of the requirement and require a recorded decision for every plausible match.
	

1. Purpose
Future Energy has identified a need to prevent unnecessary purchases where the organization already owns suitable parts or materials. The requested capability is a procurement-demand validation control placed ahead of tender optimization, bid evaluation and award approval.
The capability must not assume that any inventory record is usable. It must distinguish physical availability from technical suitability, quality evidence, project ownership, reservation status and availability by the required date.
2. Scope
In scope. The prototype must search simulated inventory and related records, identify candidate matches, assess their potential usability, create validation actions, record user decisions and provide an auditable conclusion on whether the requested procurement quantity remains necessary.
Out of scope. The prototype is not required to execute an ERP inventory transfer, change financial ownership between projects, perform physical inspection, issue a warehouse instruction, or autonomously cancel or reduce a tender.
3. Position in the procurement workflow
Control point
	System action
	Required outcome
	Requirement creation
	Search owned, reserved and expected inventory against the requested part, specification, quantity and required date.
	Show exact and potential matches before an ITT is drafted.
	Before ITT issue
	Refresh inventory availability and the disposition of identified matches.
	Prevent issue until all candidate matches have a recorded decision.
	Before award approval
	Run a final time-stamped availability check.
	Include the inventory-validation result in the award recommendation.
	

Control principle  A previous inventory search must not remain valid indefinitely. The validity period should be configurable because stock, reservations, receipts and project demand can change.
	

4. User journey
* The procurement user enters or imports the requirement, including part description, quantity, required date and technical specification reference.
* Compass searches the connected inventory, material-master, project-reservation and expected-receipt data available to the prototype.
* Compass presents exact matches and potential equivalents separately, with the evidence supporting each match.
* The user records a disposition for every candidate: use inventory, use part of inventory, request validation, retain for another purpose, or reject the match with a reason.
* Compass recalculates the residual procurement quantity but does not change it without an authorized user decision.
* Before ITT issue and award approval, Compass confirms that the check remains current and that no candidate match is unresolved.
5. Matching and validation requirements
A material-number match alone is insufficient. The search and assessment should use the following fields when they are available in the source data.
Dimension
	Fields or tests
	Identity
	Exact part or material number; superseded and successor numbers; manufacturer; model; description.
	Technical fit
	Specification, dimensions, rating, material, tolerances, approved equivalent or substitute status.
	Quantity
	On-hand quantity, reserved quantity, available-to-transfer quantity and residual requirement.
	Timing
	Required-at-site date, expected receipt date, inspection time and transfer lead time.
	Condition
	New, used, refurbished, preserved, damaged, quarantined or requiring inspection.
	Quality evidence
	Certificates, serial or batch traceability, inspection status, shelf life and preservation records.
	Control
	Storage location, owning project, reservation, transfer restrictions and responsible owner.
	Economics
	Transfer, inspection, recertification and transport costs where data is available.
	

6. Classification of search results
Classification
	Definition
	Default next action
	Available and compliant
	Unreserved inventory meets the stated technical, quality and timing requirements.
	Propose use and calculate residual quantity.
	Partially available
	Compliant available quantity is below the requirement.
	Propose reducing the procurement quantity.
	Potential substitute
	A non-identical item may meet the requirement but needs engineering or quality approval.
	Create validation action.
	Available but reserved
	Inventory is committed to another project or purpose.
	Create allocation decision action.
	Available but not ready
	Inspection, recertification, refurbishment, preservation review or transport is required.
	Compare readiness date with required date.
	Not suitable
	A stated requirement is not met.
	Record the failed criterion and exclude.
	No inventory identified
	No candidate was returned by the configured search.
	Allow procurement to continue.
	

7. Action Centre behavior
Each unresolved candidate match must create or update one procurement-validation action associated with the relevant requirement or bid package. The card should display the information needed to act without requiring the user to search elsewhere.
Card field
	Required content
	Requirement
	Package reference, requested part, requested quantity and required date.
	Candidate inventory
	Part, quantity, location, condition, reservation status and expected availability.
	Match basis
	Exact fields and source records supporting the match.
	Outstanding checks
	Technical, quality, ownership, timing or cost questions still unresolved.
	Owner and due date
	Person or function responsible for the next decision and the requested completion date.
	Available actions
	Use inventory; use partial quantity; request technical or quality validation; retain full procurement quantity.
	

If the user retains the full procurement quantity despite an identified match, Compass must require a reason. Example reasons include committed to another project, certification incomplete, required date cannot be met, technical mismatch, contingency stock must be retained, or transfer and readiness cost exceeds the purchase alternative.
8. Decision and quantity logic
Residual quantity  Residual procurement quantity = requested quantity - approved quantity from existing inventory. The result must not be lower than zero.
	

Compass may recommend a revised quantity, but an authorized user must confirm the change. The original requirement, revised quantity, decision maker, timestamp and reason must remain in the audit history.
Potential financial avoidance must not be presented as realized savings. Where a value is shown, Compass must state the calculation baseline, currency, quantity assumed and cost elements included. Until inventory use is approved, use the label Identified purchase-avoidance opportunity.
9. Workflow states
State
	Entry condition
	Exit condition
	Check required

	Search completed.
	Review required

10. Approval control
An award recommendation must include a procurement-demand validation summary containing the last check date and time, systems and locations searched, matches identified, usable quantity approved, residual procurement quantity, unresolved items and reasons any identified inventory was not used.
Blocking rule  The award cannot be submitted for approval if the inventory check is expired or any candidate match remains unresolved. An authorized override may be provided only if the prototype includes an explicit override role, a required reason and an audit entry.
	

11. Traceability and audit requirements
* Every match must link to the inventory and specification records used to produce it.
* The interface must distinguish extracted facts from system inference and user decisions.
* Every disposition must record actor, timestamp, reason and supporting evidence.
* Changes in quantity, reservation status or suitability must be retained as events rather than overwriting the prior decision history.
* The final ITT and award record must preserve the requested quantity, quantity supplied from inventory and residual purchased quantity.

Synthetic dataset

13. Acceptance criteria
ID
	Acceptance criterion

	AC-02

	AC-04
	Every plausible match requires a disposition before the ITT can be issued.

	AC-07

	AC-08

	AC-09

	AC-10

	AC-11

15. Demonstration scenario
A requirement is created for 60 subsea J-tube seals. Compass identifies 18 unreserved units in Rotterdam with matching dimensions and material specifications, but the certificates and preservation records require review. Compass creates a quality-validation action. If the units are approved, the proposed tender quantity changes from 60 to 42 after user confirmation. If they are not approved, the user records the reason and the tender remains at 60. The decision and evidence appear in the ITT and award audit history.
